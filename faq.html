What is Mīere and what makes it special?

Mīere is a wellness brand built on one simple idea — healthy should feel natural. Our honey comes from licensed New Zealand hives and is UMF™ 20+ certified, so every sachet delivers real potency and clean energy. It’s a small, golden habit that fits into busy lives — before work, post-run, or with a calming afternoon tea.

About

Is Mīere’s honey really UMF™ certified?

Yes. Every batch is independently tested and verified by the Unique Manuka Factor Honey Association (UMFHA) in New Zealand. UMF™ confirms origin, purity, and strength (including MGO levels). We publish our active certificate on-site so you can verify it yourself — full transparency, always.

Certification

What are the benefits of taking Manuka honey daily?

Manuka honey supports everyday wellness with natural antibacterial activity and antioxidants. Many customers use a daily sachet to bolster immunity, soothe digestion, and get a gentle, sustained lift without the sugar crash. It’s also a popular add-on for recovery days and skin-soothing routines.

Wellness

How should I use Mīere honey?

Tear, squeeze, enjoy. Have it straight, or stir into coffee, matcha, or smoothies. Drizzle over oats or toast, or take it pre-run for natural energy. The single-serve sachet keeps it mess-free and travel-friendly — wellness you’ll actually remember to use.

Wellness

Is Mīere suitable for children or vegans?

Mīere is 100% pure honey with no additives. Because honey is animal-derived, it isn’t vegan. As with all honey, it should not be given to children under 1 year old. For others, it’s a simple, natural way to sweeten and support your day.
