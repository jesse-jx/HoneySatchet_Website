Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Mānuka Co. (“we”, “our”, “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you share with us when visiting our website or making a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you make a purchase or sign up for our membership.
  • Payment information processed securely by our payment partners (we do not store credit card details).
  • Website usage data such as pages visited, time spent, and browser information to help us improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations, updates, and customer support messages.
  • To provide updates on new products, promotions, or membership offers (only if you consent).
  • To improve our website experience and tailor our offerings to your preferences.

3. Data Protection

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Access to personal data is restricted to employees and partners who need it to fulfill orders or provide customer service.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (e.g., shipping companies, payment processors) strictly for order fulfillment and business operations.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and track website traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, but some site features may not function properly without them.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, please contact us at support@manukaco.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any urgent questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
Email: support@manukaco.com
